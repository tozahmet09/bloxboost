# Spawnswap: one-page plan

| Field | Answer |
|---|---|
| **One sentence** | A small cinema for brand-new Roblox games: players watch trailers and discover games early, developers get their first real, verified players cheaply. |
| **Promise** | **Discovery.** Players find something new before everyone else; developers' games finally get seen. |
| **Why this** | Roblox Ads Manager only works at ~$50/day, agencies cost $1.5k+/month, shoutouts can't be measured. Here $5 buys 50 players who each played 10+ minutes, verified in-game, with written feedback. Or it costs nothing: play other devs' games to earn credits. |
| **Core loop** | 10 s: watch a trailer, tap Play. 5 min: play a game, leave feedback, earn a credit. Session: spend credits on your own game and watch verified players and reviews come in. |
| **Wow moment** | The homepage opens straight into a playing trailer. For devs: the first verified player and their review arriving on the dashboard. |
| **Variety** | New games and trailers every day; featured spotlight rotates on every visit. |
| **Return reason** | New games to discover; reviews and players arriving for your own game. No streaks, no daily tasks, nothing expires that the user loses. |
| **Look** | "Marquee": a dark cinema. Warm aubergine-black, cream text, marquee-amber for the one main action, verified-green only for verified status. Bricolage Grotesque for display, Figtree for text. Trailers are the visuals. |
| **Screens** | Discover (home), Play & earn, My games, Game setup, Credits, Link Roblox. Credits always live top right in the nav. |
| **MVP** | Built: trailers home, ad-swap credits, tracker script, feedback, credit packs, featured slots. |
| **Later** | Pro subscription, genre filters, player profiles, Robux-free gifting, funnels dashboard. |

## Rules applied (from the Product Rules vault)
- No pressure: no countdowns, no pop-up offers, shop opens only from the credits button.
- Visible next goal: every game shows progress toward its first 100 verified players.
- Feedback on key actions: earning a credit counts up in the nav with a burst.
- One visual system, no emoji icons, no card-everywhere layout.
